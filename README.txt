shanked the card game but digital


game rules:
    shuffle 2 decks of playing cards
    all players 3 cards for their main hand, 3 cards for their shown cards, and 3 cards for hidden cards that are not seen by the players
    before any cards are placed on the play pile, players are free to swap cards from their hand and their shown cards

    
    play a higher or equal card on the play pile
    you may play multiple cards of the same value
    ace is highest
    if the play pile is empty any card may be played
    wilds may be played on any card
    special cards:
        2: (wild) 
        7: next card must be equal or lower
        8: (wild) use the value of the previous card
        10: (wild) the play pile is cleared
        Jack: for each jack skip the next players turn
        Joker: all players rotate hands
    if the play pile is cleared when it's your turn, you play again 
        otherwise it's the next players turn
    if there are 4 or more cards of the same value in a row clear the pile
        8s may be between the cards but don't count them unless the 8s are the ones in a row
    if player has cards in their hand they must play from their hand
    else if player has shown cards they play one of them
    else if player only has hidden cards they must pick a card before seeing it and play it
    if the card is not playable the player is considered shanked and must move all the cards in the play pile to their hand and it's the next player's turn
    if you have no cards you win
